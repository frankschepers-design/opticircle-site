Rules with dates

Three deadlines already set.

None of these is a proposal. Each is law or an exchange rule, and in each case the data falls on the companies that make and supply the product.

18 Feb 2027EU battery passport

Every LMT, EV and large industrial battery

Batteries placed on the EU market need a digital passport. Much of the data sits with suppliers, many of them outside Europe.

Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, Art. 77

12 Aug 2026EU packaging rules

The PPWR now applies across the EU

Labelling follows from 2028, recyclability and recycled content from 2030. Buyers are already asking suppliers for packaging data.

European Commission, 11 Aug 2026

The ESG template, in English
