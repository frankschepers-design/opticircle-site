EU packaging recycling targets, and the landfill cap

Packaging, 202565%
Packaging, 203070%
Plastic, 202550%
Plastic, 203055%
Landfill cap, 203510%

All-packaging rates: at least 65% by weight by 31 December 2025 and 70% by 31 December 2030. Plastic packaging: 50% by 2025 and 55% by 2030, the material targets in Article 52 of Regulation (EU) 2025/40. The landfill line is a cap on municipal waste landfilled, not a packaging target.

Sources: European Parliament procedure summary of Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PDF) · Eurostat, municipal waste statistics (65% packaging by the end of 2025) · EUR-Lex summary, packaging and packaging waste from 2026 · European Commission, landfill waste (10% by 2035)

Two global waste outlooks, kept separate

World Bank, What a Waste 2.0: an estimated 2.01 billion tonnes of municipal solid waste in 2016, rising to 3.40 billion tonnes by 2050 under business as usual. At least 33% was not managed in an environmentally safe way.

UNEP and ISWA, Global Waste Management Outlook 2024, key findings as published on the report page: municipal solid waste generation predicted to grow from 2.1 billion tonnes in 2023 to 3.8 billion tonnes by 2050. Direct waste-management cost in 2020 about USD 252 billion; with pollution, health and climate costs, USD 361 billion. Without urgent action the page puts the 2050 annual cost at USD 640.3 billion. Waste prevention and management could limit net annual costs to USD 270.2 billion; the circular-economy scenario is described as a full net gain of USD 108.5 billion a year.

Sources: World Bank, What a Waste 2.0 (PDF) · UNEP, Global Waste Management Outlook 2024

EPR outside Europe

Rules in other regions.

Statutory targets only. No performance claim is added.

  • IndiaPlastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2022. Minimum recycling of plastic packaging waste, as a share of the EPR obligation: 30–50% in 2024–25, 40–60% in 2025–26, 50–70% in 2026–27, and 60–80% from 2027–28. The range is across packaging categories. Press Information Bureau
  • JapanContainers and Packaging Recycling Act: separate collection and recycling of containers and packaging. This index page does not state a national percentage, so none is shown. Ministry of the Environment
  • ChileLaw 20.920 (2016) establishes extended producer responsibility. Packaging collection and recovery targets apply from 16 September 2023. The pages checked do not quote the percentage targets, so none are shown. Law 20.920 · Ministry of the Environment, packaging
  • CaliforniaSB 54 requires that not less than 65% of plastic covered material be recycled on and after 1 January 2032. California Legislative Information, SB 54
  • Hong KongOne entry among these, not the frame for the page. MSW recovery 33% in 2023; 3.97 million tonnes of MSW disposed of at landfills. Charging is a reserve tool, not in force. EPD via news.gov.hk · GovHK

No single “typical” Lean cost reduction is shown. The studies that were checked report plant-level results, not one percentage that can be used as a benchmark, and this page does not invent one.

Savings calculator

Indicative estimate, not a guarantee.

Assumes the tonnage you enter is currently all sent to disposal, and that the target diversion is reached in full. Savings = tonnes diverted × (disposal cost per tonne − net recycling cost per tonne). Enter a positive recycling figure if you pay, a negative figure if recycling pays you. Payback = upfront cost ÷ annual saving, and only when that saving is positive.
