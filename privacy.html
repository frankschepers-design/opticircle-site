Privacy note

Privacy note

Last updated 31 October 2026

This note explains what personal data this website and Opticircle Consulting Limited ("Opticircle", "we") handle, and why. We aim to comply with Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) and with the privacy laws of the places where our clients are based.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. The site has no contact forms, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

Like any website, the hosting provider that serves these pages records technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Those logs are kept by the host under its own privacy policy, and we do not use them to identify visitors.

When you email or call us

If you email or phone us, we receive your name, contact details and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply, to prepare a proposal and, if you become a client, to deliver and invoice the work. Our email runs on Microsoft 365. We do not sell or share your details for marketing.

Please do not send confidential or sensitive documents in a first email. Clients share files only after a non-disclosure agreement and through a channel we agree together.

Client data and AI tools

We use AI tools to read regulations and client files. Client data is used only for that client's engagement, is processed under business terms that exclude training of third-party models, and is reviewed by a person before any finding is signed.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 24 months. Client records are kept as long as Hong Kong accounting and tax law requires, then deleted.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email frankschepers@opticircle.com with the subject "Personal data request" and we will reply within 40 days, the period the Ordinance sets for access requests.

Who we are

Opticircle Consulting Limited, a company registered in Hong Kong (Business Registration No. 76628464-000), managed from Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia.
