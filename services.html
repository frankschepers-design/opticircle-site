The Opticircle method

One loop, four stages.

  • AuditReadiness checks, the ESG baseline, waste and circularity audits, second opinions.
  • AdviseA gap list cited to the rule, and a 60-day plan with owners and dates.
  • ImplementSupplier data requests, ISO 14001 support, management systems and training for your team.
  • ReportThe ESG report package and the compliance retainer: reports and board notes, signed.

Method

How a readiness check runs.

Two weeks, remote. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share anything sensitive.

  1. Kick-off callYour products, your markets and the rules that apply to them.
  2. Data requestOne list of what we need. You send what you have.
  3. Gap reviewAI reads the rules and your files. We check every line.
  4. FindingsEach gap cited to its rule and signed by a named person.
  5. 60-day planWho does what, in what order, before the deadline.
