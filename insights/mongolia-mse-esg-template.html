Mongolia's listed companies and the ESG template: getting the first report right

Frank Schepers · 31 October 2026

Since October 2024, Tier I issuers on the Mongolian Stock Exchange have had to submit the exchange's ESG reporting template by 30 April of the following year, in English. For the 2026 financial year, that means 30 April 2027. Tier II and III issuers may report voluntarily.

How the first rounds went

A study published in October 2026 in the Journal of Economics, Finance and Accounting Studies looked at the reports filed so far. It found that on-time filing across Tier I was far from complete, and that first reports ran to a median of seven pages. The template asks for sustainability management, environmental, social and governance indicators, plus sector-specific ones; where data is missing, the company has to explain why and what it will do about it.

Why it matters beyond the exchange

Banks, foreign buyers and investors read these reports. A thin report invites more questions, and a late one is visible in a public registry. The national roadmap for IFRS sustainability standards also points to wider, stricter reporting in the years ahead, so a weak data system built now will have to be rebuilt.

What to do before 30 April

  • Name one owner for the report and agree the data calendar with finance now, not in March.
  • Pull energy, water, waste and workforce data from the systems you already have, and note where it is estimated.
  • Answer every template indicator; where data is missing, give the reason and the plan, as the template requires.
  • Write it in English from the start rather than translating at the end.
