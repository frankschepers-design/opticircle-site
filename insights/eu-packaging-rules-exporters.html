EU packaging rules now apply. What exporters outside Europe will be asked

Frank Schepers · 31 October 2026

The EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) has applied across the EU since 12 August 2026. It replaces a patchwork of national rules with one regulation that covers packaging from design to waste.

What applies when

  • Since 12 August 2026: the regulation applies, including a restriction on PFAS in food-contact packaging above strict limits.
  • From 2028: a harmonised EU labelling system to help consumers sort waste.
  • From 2030: limits on empty space, restrictions on certain single-use plastic packaging, reuse targets, mandatory recycled content in plastic packaging, and the requirement that all packaging is recyclable.

Why a supplier outside the EU should care

The legal duties fall on the companies that place packaging on the EU market. In practice they pass the questions down the chain. If you sell packed goods into Europe, expect your buyer to ask what each pack is made of, what it weighs, whether it contains restricted substances, and how recyclable it is. The supplier with clean answers keeps the contract.

The UK is already pricing it

In the UK, packaging EPR fees for 2026-27 vary with recyclability. Packaging rated red pays 20% above the base fee, while green-rated packaging gets an illustrative 9% discount, according to GOV.UK's year 2 illustrative fees. Design decisions now show up on the invoice.

What to do this quarter

  • List every pack you ship to the EU and the UK, with materials and weights.
  • Screen each pack for recyclability and for PFAS in food-contact uses.
  • Agree with your buyers who reports what, and in which format.
