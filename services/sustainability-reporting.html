The situation

Why it matters now

Annual reporting is not a project; it is a cycle. Most companies rebuild the same spreadsheet every spring. A managed system collects the data through the year, so the report is a dry run long before the deadline.

For: Companies that must report every year and do not have a sustainability team.

Our approach

What we do

  1. Set upA data system built around the files and people you already have.
  2. CollectMonthly or quarterly data, checked as it arrives.
  3. RehearseReporting dry runs before the real deadline.
  4. ReportA quarterly board pack and the annual report, reviewed and signed.
