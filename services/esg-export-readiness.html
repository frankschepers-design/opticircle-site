The situation

Why it matters now

Foreign buyers, banks and investors now send ESG questionnaires before they sign. Tier I issuers on the Mongolian Stock Exchange file the ESG template, in English, by 30 April each year, and a 2026 study found only 53% of mandated Tier I reports arrived on time. A weak answer costs sales and raises the cost of money.

For: Companies answering ESG questions from foreign buyers, lenders and investors, and issuers preparing their first ESG report.
