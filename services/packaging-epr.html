The situation

Why it matters now

The EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation has applied since 12 August 2026, with labelling rules from 2028 and recyclability and recycled-content rules from 2030. In the UK, packaging that rates red for recyclability pays 20% above the base EPR fee in 2026-27. Buyers are passing the questions down the chain, and the supplier with clean answers keeps the contract.

For: Exporters and brands selling packed goods into the EU or the UK.

Our approach

What we do

  1. MapWhich rules apply to which packs, in which markets, and who in your chain carries each duty.
  2. InventoryMaterials, weights and substances for every pack, from the records you already hold.
  3. ScreenRecyclability, pack by pack, judged the way a recycling plant would judge it.
  4. PlanA gap list with each gap cited to its rule, and a 60-day plan.

Deliverables

What you get

  • Obligations map for the EU PPWR and UK packaging EPR
  • Packaging data inventory
  • Recyclability screen for each pack
  • Gap list, each item cited to the rule it rests on
  • 60-day plan with owners and dates
