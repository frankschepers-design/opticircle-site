What your waste really is, what it costs, and what can be recycled, from someone who has run the plant.

The situation

Why it matters now

Most sites know what they pay to get rid of waste, but not what it is. Without measured streams, ESG figures are estimates and EPR duties are guesses. The opportunities sit in the same data: material that can be sold, not paid for.

For: Factories, mines, logistics sites and retailers.

Our approach

What we do

  1. WalkA site visit to see the streams where they arise, not where they leave.
  2. MeasureStreams weighed, sampled and mapped to their destinations.
  3. JudgeWhat is really recyclable, where, and at what cost, judged with Lean methods by someone who has run sorting lines.
  4. ReportOptions to cut cost and meet packaging and EPR duties, with data your ESG report can use.

Deliverables

What you get

  • Measured waste stream map
  • Lean loss review: where material, time and money leave the process
  • Material flow and destination review
  • Recycling and cost-reduction options, ranked
  • Data formatted for your ESG report
  • An audit, not a certification: Opticircle does not issue certifications

Typical engagement

Who this is for

Illustrative example, not a client

An industrial site wants to know which of its waste streams could be recycled locally, and what that would save against current disposal costs.
