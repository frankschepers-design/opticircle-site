EU Battery Passport Readiness Check

A field-by-field check against the passport data the EU will require from 18 February 2027.

The situation

Why it matters now

From 18 February 2027, every LMT battery, EV battery and industrial battery above 2 kWh placed on the EU market needs a digital passport, registered in the EU registry. The company placing the battery on the market is responsible, but most of the data sits with suppliers, many outside Europe.

For: Makers, importers and brands of LMT, EV and industrial batteries above 2 kWh, and their non-EU suppliers.

Our approach

What we do

  1. ScopeWhich models need a passport, and who is the responsible economic operator for each.
  2. FieldsEvery data point the Commission has published, marked as held, held by a supplier, or missing.
  3. SuppliersA request pack your suppliers can actually answer, with the level of detail the Commission expects.
  4. PlanRegistry route and a 60-day plan to the deadline.

Deliverables

What you get

  • Field-by-field gap check against the published battery passport data points
  • Responsible-operator view for each battery model
  • Supplier data request pack
  • 60-day plan to 18 February 2027
