The situation

Why it matters now

Most reports are written by people who have never run a plant. The weak points are usually the same: waste figures that do not add up, recyclability claims a sorting line would reject, and EU or UK packaging duties nobody has priced. A second opinion finds them while they are still cheap to fix. Keep your current adviser; this sits alongside their work.

For: Companies with a consultant or in-house team that want an operator's check before a report goes out.

Our approach

What we do

  1. ReadYour current report, plan or consultant deliverable, and the rule or framework it answers.
  2. CheckLine by line against GRI, ISSB, the PPWR or UK EPR, as relevant.
  3. FlagGaps, unsupported claims and missing data, each cited.
  4. AdviseWhat to fix before you publish, in priority order.

Deliverables

What you get

  • Written review with each finding cited
  • List of unsupported or risky claims
  • EU and UK packaging EPR exposure note
  • Fee credited in full against any follow-on engagement within 60 days
