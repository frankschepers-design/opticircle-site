Career

From the plant floor to the boardroom.

Frank spent more than 15 years with SUEZ and SITA, responsible for recycling and waste management in Europe, latterly as Director Asset Management Benelux & Germany (2008 to 2019). At PreZero he designed and built the company's newest sorting facility in Ghent, Belgium, then started it up and managed it from 2018 to 2021. The plant in Evergem opened on 17 February 2021: about 80,000 tonnes a year, a €46 million investment and 26 NIR sorters (PreZero, public plant figures).

As general manager of the Recycled PET division at Swire Coca-Cola, covering Southeast Asia and China, he led strategies for bringing 30 to 50 percent recycled PET into packaging. He also served as general manager of New Life Plastics, a joint venture of Swire Coca-Cola, ALBA Group and Baguio Waste Management and Recycling. Its 6,500 square metre plant at EcoPark in Tuen Mun, officially opened in December 2022, was the largest food-grade-ready plastic recycling facility in Hong Kong, built to process up to 900 tonnes of PET a month.

He now lives and works in Mongolia. He is general director of TML Plastic Mongolia, which recycles PET bottles into food-grade rPET pellets, and is preparing Pyrotec, a plastic-to-oil project in Ulaanbaatar, for which he wrote the business plan, the ESG paper and the bank financing file. He is also chief executive of Mongolia Without Waste, a Mongolian NGO. In Hong Kong he has also advised as a senior consultant with Weplastic. He speaks regularly on circular packaging, including at the Hong Kong Productivity Council's international conference on the plastics industry in September 2025.

Frank earned his Lean Black Belt at Cardiff University in 2019. He uses it where most compliance advisers stop: on plant performance, sorting-line throughput and yield, and taking cost out of operations, so the data a regulator asks for comes from a process that is under control.

Opticircle is how he puts that experience to work for companies facing a compliance deadline: fast, fixed-fee, and signed by someone who has had to make the numbers work.
