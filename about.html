About Frank

Frank Schepers

Managing Director, Opticircle Consulting. Lean Black Belt (Cardiff University, 2019). Belgian. Based in Ulaanbaatar.

Frank has worked inside recycling and waste management for his whole career, and has designed, built and run the plants himself. He knows compliance from the side most advisers never see: the plant floor, where a "recyclable" claim either survives the sorting line or does not.

Career

From the plant floor to the boardroom.

Frank spent more than 15 years with SUEZ and SITA, responsible for recycling and waste management in Europe, latterly as Director Asset Management Benelux & Germany (2008 to 2019). At PreZero he designed and built the company's newest sorting facility in Ghent, Belgium, then started it up and managed it from 2018 to 2021. The plant in Evergem opened on 17 February 2021: about 80,000 tonnes a year, a €46 million investment and 26 NIR sorters (PreZero, public plant figures).

He now lives and works in Ulaanbaatar, on UTC+8, which covers the Asian working day and the European morning. Opticircle Consulting Limited is registered in Hong Kong.

Frank earned his Lean Black Belt at Cardiff University in 2019. He uses it where most compliance advisers stop: on plant performance, sorting-line throughput and yield, and taking cost out of operations, so the data a regulator asks for comes from a process that is under control.

Opticircle is how he puts that experience to work for companies facing a compliance deadline: fast, fixed-fee, and signed by someone who has had to make the numbers work.
